Every item your team receives has a proof record — a live HTTP response confirming the exploit, a DNS callback hit, or a symbolic execution oracle. Nothing is assumed. Everything is demonstrated.

Universal proof gate

No candidate becomes a finding without deterministic live evidence. Required by policy for every scan mode — cannot be bypassed.

Red vs Blue AI debate

A separate AI model using a different architecture actively challenges every high-confidence claim. Disputed findings are marked, not buried.

🧬

Patched-twin comparison

Run against a known-fixed "twin" target. Any finding that survives a patched version is automatically flagged as a potential false positive.

📋

Full evidence trail

Every finding includes: hypothesis, exact tool call, raw response, validator reasoning, and proof record. Fully reproducible by your own engineers.

The executive message: GENESIS does not ask you to trust the AI. It asks you to trust the evidence — the same HTTP response, callback hit, or proof record the AI used. The entire reasoning chain is visible, auditable, and independently reproducible by your team.
